Logo usage notes

The logo is always linked to the homepage. On the hero/fullscreen slider the transparent navbar shows the monochrome inverse variant. Once the page scrolls past 100px, the sticky ("ragadós") navbar locks to the top and shows the colour RGB variant on a white background. The mobile logo is the colour RGB variant at 50px height.

06 — Design Principles

Motion & Animation

  • Navbar sticky transition: 0.5s ease-in-out
  • Page preloader with spinner animation
  • Hero slider: LayerSlider, 7000ms per slide, transition-2D type 11
  • Slide-in elements: durationIn 700ms, delay 200–700ms
  • Smooth scroll: enabled for Chrome via smoothscroll.js

Layout & Grid

  • Full-width layout above 1000px (max-width: 100%)
  • Content container: max-width 1200px, centred
  • Bootstrap 4 grid (12-column), plus custom 5-column (20% each)
  • Section padding: generally 60–80px vertical
  • No left/right borders above 1000px

Tone & Language

  • Bilingual: Hungarian (primary) + English
  • All-caps headlines, tight letter-spacing
  • Industry keywords: NFC/RFID, cashless, integrated, real-time
  • Uppercase navigation links, 1px letter-spacing

Photography Style

  • Festival crowd shots, outdoor events, wristband close-ups
  • Dark overlay (rgba 0,0,0,0.5) on hero images
  • Hero text boxes on brand-teal (#274B5C) background
  • Team photos: 250×250px square crops, neutral bg
